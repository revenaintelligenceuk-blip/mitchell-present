Qualified three times over

  • Psychotherapist. Fully qualified, and trusted with young people's hardest moments.

  • Life coach. Helping people believe in what's possible for them again.

Two organisations, built from scratch

Programmes that change lives

  • Support in schools. In-school mentoring and counselling that improve attendance and reduce exclusions.
